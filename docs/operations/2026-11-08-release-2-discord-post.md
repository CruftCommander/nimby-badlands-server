````markdown
**NIMBY release 2 is live** (November 8, 2026)

The server restarted at 1:22 PM Central with the new @NIMBY pack. Your launcher will update NIMBY the next time you join; if it says the mod is out of date, restart your launcher (DZSA or the DayZ Launcher) and let it download (about 10 MB).

**What's new**

**DPV.** A desert patrol buggy at military sites, four to six on the map, each on its wheels. Find a battery, spark plug, bulbs and fuel to get one moving. The side rails carry jerry cans, ammo cans, packs and tents, the roof takes a wooden crate, a car tent or a teddy bear, and the cabin holds a first aid kit. Look at an item on the car to take it. Near 140 km/h (87 mph) flat out, so mind the wadis. The DPV took over some of the Humvee spots, so Humvees turn up in fewer places.

**Loot.** The Oasis tents no longer rain golden guns. The golden AK and its mags, the golden Deagle and Magnum are a handful per map, counted wherever they are (including in your hands), and slow to come back. FAL and wooden VSD one at a time. Plate carriers and the Akinaka are rarer. Night vision now comes only from the active bunker's crate. The 1911 family and binoculars are half as common as before.

**Hands.** Handing an item to another player works again. Hold it and press O to offer; empty hands and O asks for theirs.

**Greet.** The welcome window has a "What's changed" tab with this list. Insert reopens it.

Everything is in one Workshop item, NIMBY: <https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828109139>
Source and notes: <https://github.com/CruftCommander/nimby-badlands-server>

Found a DPV stuck in a rock, a note that will not pin, or loot that feels off? Post it in <#1562081423870009345> with a screenshot and the nearest town.
````
