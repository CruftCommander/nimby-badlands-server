# :tools: NIMBY Mod Roadmap
-# Updated Oct 27, 2026 · Every mod here is built in-house by Road Dog and ships in one Workshop item, `@NIMBY`

**The first wave is live.** `@NIMBY` is required to join: <https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828109139>. Subscribe once; the launcher keeps it current. Every NIMBY keybinding can be rebound under the **NIMBY** tab in Controls. New mods go live only after testing on a private copy of the live server. Source is public: <https://github.com/CruftCommander/nimby-badlands-server>

**Why build our own?** When Badlands dropped, the usual Workshop mods had no 1.30 builds. In-house, we fix things ourselves after every update, everything fits the hardcore "vanilla-plus, never easier" style, and you subscribe to one item.

**Stages:** Planning → Feasibility → Planned → Building → Built → Testing → Tested → Live
-# Feasibility is a short study against the 1.30 engine before building; some ideas stop there. **Patching** means a live mod has a known fault and its fix is in testing. **Priority** (High, Medium, Low, Very low) is what gets worked on first.

## :white_check_mark: Live (first wave, Oct 27, 2026)
**:leg: Legs**: Autorun. Double-tap Left Shift to auto-run/auto-swim; any movement input takes control back.
**:ear: Ears**: Earplugs on one key (N). Voice chat isn't affected.
**:writing_hand: Scrawl**: Notes with pen and paper. Paper stacks to 5, notes hold 512 characters, untouched notes disappear after 30 days.
**:pushpin: Tack**: Pin a note to a wall with a hammer and nail, duct tape, epoxy putty, or a knife into wood. Ripping one down damages the sheet.
**:wave: Greet**: The welcome window with rules, how-tos and restart times. Insert reopens it.
`▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰` Live

## :wrench: Patching (live, but broken)
**:handshake: Hands**: Hand what you're holding to another player (O to offer), ask for theirs, or open your pockets.
Right now hand-offs don't complete: the animation plays, nothing moves, nothing is lost. The fix is built and in testing for the next update; until then, drop items for each other. Tracking: <https://github.com/CruftCommander/nimby-badlands-server/issues/6>
`▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱` Patching · Priority: High
-# Coming after: open pockets let others look in, add, and take (your pockets, your risk).

## :toolbox: Next in line
**:detective: Central Scrutinizer**: In-house admin tool for the staff, every action logged. Replaces COT.
`▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱` Planned · Priority: High

## :smoking: The smoke shop (in this order)
**Puff**: Cigarette packs and lighters, rolling, a pipe, joints, hash, and a water shisha.
**Buzz**: What smoking does to you. Effects stack and wear off; expect coughing and laughing.
**Sprout**: Grow tobacco and cannabis, dry it, and cook hash over a fire.
`▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱` Planned · Priority: Medium
-# Three mods, so an update that breaks the effects leaves the items and crops working.

## :mag: Studied (feasible, not yet planned)
**Shoulder**: A rifle rides on your back only with a sling; make one from a rope. Short rifles can strap to a backpack. Medium
**Bikes**: Two riders on the motorbikes, saddlebags on the dirt bike, a basket on the moped. Medium
**Tweaker**: Dust storms dehydrate you faster, fuel from wrecked tankers, cactus scrapes bleed. Medium
**Beards**: Beards grow longer than vanilla allows. Very low (hard to do well)
`▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱` Feasibility

## :bulb: On the drawing board (ideas, no promises)
**Splash**: A custom NIMBY loading screen. Low
**Payload**: Wire explosives into a vehicle; set them off with the horn or a remote. Low
**Cassettes**: Play music with a radio, a battery, and a tape. Medium
**Face paint**: Ash from a dead fire, or guts for a blood face. Low
**Eyes**: Behind-the-scenes tooling for the admin team. Low (High after Central Scrutinizer)
**Small ideas**: Cut the rope on a dug well; waterboarding a restrained player. Low
`▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱` Planning
