## What changed between 164078 and 164200

`diff -rq` of the two extracts: 86 files differ, 4 paths exist only in 164200 (two of them case-only renames of `ak101` and `ak74` to `AK101` and `AK74`, plus `scripts/1_Core/DayZ/WorkbenchAPI/` and `ConstructionPartRebuildInvertedSupport.c`), 2 only in 164078 (the lower-case weapon folders). No file cited by Plan 3 v0.7, the two Central Scrutinizer feasibility documents, or the Phase 0 recon changed, except `InventoryItem.c` (addendum F18, spectate; three same-line edits, no line shift). Files the later phases will read and that did change: `ActionRestrainTarget.c` (N11; gains `IsLockTargetOnUse`, 5 lines), `MiscGameplayFunctions.c` (209 lines, 2,139 to 2,098), `Transport.c` and `CarScript.c` (Phase 5 vehicle registry), `CCTCursor.c` (object selection, 37 to 45 lines), `PluginManager.c`, `Debug.c`. A plan that cites one of these re-reads the line before use.
