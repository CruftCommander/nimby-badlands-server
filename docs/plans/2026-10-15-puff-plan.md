# Plan 4 - Puff (`NimbySmoke`)

Draft v0.2, October 15, 2026. Owner: Jeff Fabish. Implementer: a Claude Code session in worktree `feat/puff`, coordinated by the orchestrator. Status: draft for Jeff's review; implementation waits on the shared rules v1.1 amendment (content addons) being on `main`.

Target: NIMBY | HARDCORE | BADLANDS | 1pp, HostHavoc, DayZ 1.30.164078 / Nasdara.
Release: independent, gated (see [shared rules, 9.2](2026-10-15-shared-rules.md)), plus the content-addon gate in [shared rules, 6.1](2026-10-15-shared-rules.md).
Feasibility: `docs/design/puff-sprout-scrawl-earplugs-feasibility.md`; where the two differ, this plan is authoritative.

Changes since v0.1: effects, symptoms, the cloud trigger, and night vision moved to Buzz (Plan 8) under the split-by-what-breaks decision (D9); Puff keeps the items and raises a puff event that Buzz consumes; the hash item stays here, hash production moves to Sprout Phase 4 (cooking, D15).

Puff is the smoking items: cigarette packs that may hold a lighter, loose cigarettes that only come from packs or rolling, a pipe, joints, hash, and a water shisha. Everything lights with a vanilla `PetrolLighter` or `Matchbox`, burns down on a timer, and raises one script event per puff. Puff has no opinion about what a puff does to the player; that is Buzz, and Puff works with Buzz absent.

## What this is not

- Not the effects. No post-process, symptom, stat, trigger, or particle code lives here (Buzz).
- Not a persistence change beyond items. Puff defines no `OnStoreSave`.
- Not a custom-animation mod. Puffing reuses a vanilla consume animation command chosen in Phase 0.
- Not a modification of vanilla classes. `CigarettePack_*`, `PetrolLighter`, `Matchbox`, `Cannabis`, and `Paper` are used as they are; Puff defines its own pack classes on the vanilla pack model.
- Not dependent on Buzz. No Puff script references a Buzz class; Buzz subscribes to Puff's event.

## Phase 1 - cigarettes on placeholders

- Addon skeleton: `config.cpp` (`CfgPatches` requiring `DZ_Data`, `DZ_Gear_Medical`, `DZ_Gear_Cooking`, `DZ_Gear_Consumables`, `DZ_Gear_Food`), `Scripts/3_Game` (constants, the puff event), `Scripts/4_World` (items, actions), string table, `NOTICE` with our own license.
- Items: `NimbySmoke_CigarettePack_ColorBase` plus three brands (P2); `NimbySmoke_Cigarette` with quantity = puffs and a burning net-synced flag; `NimbySmoke_TobaccoLeaf` (an `Edible_Base` with raw and dried stages, loot-only until Sprout grows it) so Sprout and the Phase 2 recipes have the class to reference.
- Actions: take a cigarette from a pack (pack quantity minus one, cigarette to hands); light (vanilla ignition contract, consumes igniter quantity as vanilla does); puff (continuous, one puff per completed cycle, decrements quantity, raises the event); put out.
- Burn timer: a lit cigarette loses one puff per configured burn interval whether or not it is smoked; at zero it deletes itself with a `[NIMBY][Smoke]` line.
- Self-test (shared rules 8): burn arithmetic, event payload validation, config class names resolve.
- `pack.json` row added by the orchestrator; `types.xml` rows drafted in `server/` but not applied live.
