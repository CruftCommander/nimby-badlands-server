Tack pins a sheet of paper to a wall with a hammer and a nail, with duct tape, or with epoxy putty, and lets anyone pull it down by hand at the cost of some damage to the sheet. It is separate from Scrawl because pinning is a raycast, a placement, and a physics override, all script-API-heavy, while Scrawl is a config change; a Bohemia update that breaks placement takes Tack down and leaves notes and stacks working.

## What this is not

- Not a note system and not a paper stack. Writing and reading are vanilla; the stack is Scrawl.
- Not a base-building piece. A pinned note is not attached to the wall entity and does not follow a wall that is dismantled; it stays where it was pinned until pulled down or despawned.
- Not a new persistence field on `Paper`. The pinned state is a class of its own (T3), not data on the vanilla class.
- Not a staple gun. Hammer plus nail, duct tape, and epoxy putty are the fasteners in this plan; a staple gun is a later item if Jeff wants one.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Simulation re-enables after load and the sheet falls | Phase 0 item 4; the class re-disables in init; fallback is a static (non-item) class holding the text, which costs its own persistence |
| A ruined note loses its text | Phase 0 item 5; if so, the default damage drops so a sheet survives more pulls |
| Players paper every wall | Per-area cap (T6) and the 30-day lifetime |
| A Bohemia update breaks raycast or placement | Tack is removed from `pack.json`; Scrawl and vanilla notes keep working |
