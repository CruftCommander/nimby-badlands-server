## Findings

- **NIMBY-T-069 (closed, build 18).** Engine stopped within 1 s of starting: the periodic check at `CarScript.c:848-854` read `m_EngineHealth <= 0` because `PartsHealthCheck` derives it from `GetHealthLevelValue(GetHealthLevel("Engine"), "Engine")` and the DPV declared no `healthLevels[]`. Every ADA zone and its `GlobalHealth` carry a five-level table; the DPV now has it on every zone. The wheel half of T-069 (proxy class is `Proxy` + proxy model file name) was closed at build 14.
- **NIMBY-T-080 (closed, build 19).** No engine or tyre sound. Vanilla cars get their sound sets from `class Sounds { soundSetsFilter[]; soundSetsInt[]; }` on the vehicle class in the `DZ_Sounds_Effects` pbo (`D:\DayZProjects\DZ\sounds\hpp\config.cpp:519352`), not from `wheeled.config.cpp`. The DPV carries the ADA's Offroad sets; `DZ_Sounds_Effects` is in `requiredAddons[]`.
- **NIMBY-T-081 (closed, build 20).** Battery and radiator hung outside the rear frame: both were placed at x = +-0.45, y = rear + 0.6, where the frame narrows to +-0.45. The radiator was then removed altogether (Jeff's ruling: the DPV is air-cooled; `IsVitalRadiator()` returns false, which gates every coolant check in `CarScript`). The battery sits in the open pocket behind the passenger seat, x 0.3 to 0.55, y -1.05 to -0.85, z 0.5 to 0.75, found from a 0.1 m occupancy map of the source mesh.
- **NIMBY-T-082 (closed, build 19).** The rear seat offered no get-in prompt: `ActionGetInTransport.ActionCondition` (`:64`) needs the player within 1.0 m, measured flat, of the seat's condition point (`CarScript.CanReachSeatFromDoors`, `CarScript.c:2177`), and `seat_con_1_2` sat at the passenger-side body edge. Now on the centre line, with `NimbyDpv_Car.CanReachSeatFromDoors` giving that seat 1.8 m so both sides and behind qualify.
- **NIMBY-T-083 (closed, build 22).** No seat switch offered from inside: the seat selections were the whole seat mesh, so the seated camera sat inside its own seat's View Geometry and the aim ray hit the occupied seat. Selections are now the cushion (bottom 0.30 m), and the View Geometry is a hollow open-buggy envelope (tub to 0.62, hood deck, rear engine box, cage above 1.5) so the cushions are reachable from outside as well. Build 20's cushion-only change without the hollow envelope buried the front cushions inside the tub box; the server freeze of that run (T-092) hid the regression.
- **NIMBY-T-084 (closed, build 23).** Switching to the rear seat took the crew slot but left the body in the passenger seat (`seat=2 root x=+0.45` in the diag): `VehicleAnimInstances.HATCHBACK` has seat changes for front<->front and rear<->rear only, as vanilla `OffroadHatchback.CanReachSeatFromSeat` encodes. The DPV override now allows 0<->1 only.
- **NIMBY-T-085 (closed, build 23).** Spare could not be taken: `CarScript.CanManipulateSpareWheel()` is false by default (`CarScript.c:670`) and each vanilla car gates it on a door or trunk animation. The DPV overrides it to true.
- **NIMBY-T-086 (closed, build 25).** Spark plug ruined in minutes of light off-road driving. Not collisions: `CarScript.c:876-883` damages the Engine zone every tick at or above `rpmRedline` (`rpm * 0.001 * rand(0.02, 1.0)`; 5 per cent of engine health per tick above `rpmMax`), and Engine-zone damage reaches the 20-hitpoint plug 1:1. The DPV's engine and gearbox are the ADA's, so the cause was the suspension: 28000/6200 with 0.18/0.16 of travel against the ADA's 40000/7500 and 0.088/0.083 let the rear wheels unload on bumps and the rear-drive engine free-rev to the redline. Build 25 uses ADA stiffness and damping with travel a third longer than the ADA's; the Back zone's transfer to Engine was also cut from 0.5 to the ADA's 0.15 (build 23) and the collision box lifted to the real frame bottom (build 24), both kept. The redline damage is vanilla and still applies with the throttle held and wheels in the air.
- **NIMBY-T-087 (closed, build 25).** Swerving on gear changes at speed: the same soft suspension (wheel hop), plus a quarter of the Geometry mass in the cage box above z 1.0. Mass is now 55 per cent tub, 25 per cent tail (rear engine), 10 per cent hood, 10 per cent cage.
- **NIMBY-T-088 (closed, build 25).** Hovering about 5 cm after the mass move (build 24): per-axle sag changed and the damper animation range no longer matched. `model.cfg` damper `offset0/offset1` and the config `initPhase` must equal the axle's `travelMaxDown/Up` and `down / (down + up)`; they do now.
- **NIMBY-T-089 (procedural).** Test cars persist in `storage_998` across boots, and cars saved before a config change come back broken (`fuel=-nan`, no wheels present) and piled up. Wipe the world before a boot that changed the model or slots: `Remove-Item '<server>\mpmissions\dayzOffline.nasdara\storage_998\*' -Recurse -Force` (empty the folder; it must exist). `New-Item -Force` on an existing folder does not empty it.

Ceiling now at least 25.9 from rest. Tyre friction is exhausted as the lever; the traction-control script is next, and T-099 gives it a second job. **Build 34 (22:24):** the script is in (`NimbyDpv_Car.NimbyDpvTractionControlStep`, cap 0.3, margin max(3 m/s, 25 per cent of ground speed), rear wheels, forward gears) and proven to hold: the server-side `SetThrottle` from `EOnPostSimulate` reads back as 0.3 while W is held. - **NIMBY-T-097 (open, handling).** Build 31 (tyre 1.0/4.5/3.0): steering feels heavy, "like driving a semi". Build 31's log also ends with the car 1.1 to 1.4 m above the surface at rest after decelerating from 89 km/h in one 5 s sample, pitch rocking between -15 and +12 degrees while stationary: a probable rollover at speed. Build 30's log ends the same way after a crash at 87 km/h. 3.0 lateral grip on 1200 kg with the mass in the tub stops the car sliding and lets it tip. Candidates, one per build: lateral back to 2.5 (hold longitudinal 4.5 for the restarts), the `Steering` rates (`increaseSpeed` `{ 0, 50, 30, 30, 100, 10 }` against RearDrive's six-point curve, `maxSteeringAngle` 35), and the 950 kg mass revisit. Jeff accepted the build 31 tune for a first release at 20:50 and withdrew that at 21:11 after the build 31 run: pulls right on every shift and drives like ice on dirt and sand, which is the vehicle's whole point. **Cause found in two parts.** (1) The friction ratio was inverted: every intact vanilla wheel has lateral above longitudinal (HatchbackWheel 0.85/0.95, Hatchback_02, Offroad_02 and Truck_01 1.5/1.8, Truck_01 double 2.5/2.8) and 4.5/3.0 gave the rear tyres 1.5 times more drive grip than cornering grip on a rear-drive car with a locked diff, so each torque step let the tail go. Build 32 (3.0/3.6) removed the ice feel; the pull went from right to slight left. (2) The remaining pull is the locked rear axle (`DIFFERENTIAL_LOCKED`, which only the M3S truck uses in vanilla; ADA, Olga, Gunter and Humvee are `DIFFERENTIAL_OPEN`) on uneven grip: build 33 proved the car is level (roll 0.5 and 0.0 at rest on tarmac facing opposite ways) and holds a hands-off heading to the decimal for 20 s at up to 110 km/h, so there is no built-in asymmetry; the pull only appears on mixed ground and its direction follows the ground. Opening the diff would cost the hill restart (torque goes to the spinning wheel), so it is logged, not planned. **Rollover mechanism:** the builder's Geometry mass puts 10 per cent in the cage at 1.5 to 2.1 m and 55 per cent in the tub at 0.46 to 1.00 m, a centre of mass about 0.85 m up on a 1.88 m track, so the static rollover threshold is about 1.1 g; lateral grip above vanilla's 1.8 holds past that and tips. Lowering the centre of mass is a builder change (move the cage share into the tub), logged for the model rebuild with the 950 kg mass. Jeff, 21:32: flipping is not a concern currently. **Build 34 (22:38):** with traction control on top of the build 32 tune Jeff is happy with the handling ("very impressive"). Release acceptance ruling is section 5 of the November 7 handoff.

- **NIMBY-T-099 (open, airborne spin-up).** Build 33, 21:58 to 22:02, off-road at 70 to 90 km/h: roll to plus or minus 12, pitch to 18, then 30 to 60 degree heading swings in the 5 s after each airborne moment, rollover at 22:02:18 at 67 km/h in third.
