- Take-down (P0-5): target-registered continuous actions need `ContinuousInteractActionInput`; fixed in `ActionNimbyTackTakeDown.GetInputType()`. Rule for every NIMBY action that lives on a world object: hold F, not left mouse.
- Surfaces (P0-6): `RaycastRVResult.surface` is empty for building view geometry on 1.30; the rebuild probes fire geometry. If that is empty too, T12 decides between a wall-class allowlist and dropping the wood test.
- `AddActionJuncture` error: the pin actions locked a wall target; `m_LockTargetOnUse = false` now.
- Ignition: Ignite was offered on a pinned note and did nothing (vanilla turns a lit sheet into a fireplace and `CanIgniteEntityAsFireplace` refuses a wall).
